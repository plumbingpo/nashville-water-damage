Heavy rainstorms, hurricanes, tropical storms and flash floods can do serious water damage to homes and buildings in Nashville. Gaps in siding, broken glass and roof damage all let wind-blown rain into the building. Insulation, ceiling drywall and the hollow spaces in walls can all end up saturated by a leaking roof. Lower levels flood when storm drains are overwhelmed by a sudden surge of water. Nashville Restoration Co provides emergency storm response: we stop more water from getting in, pump out what is standing and begin restoring the property immediately.

We handle every stage of weather-related water damage: tarping and boarding up to stop further intrusion, extracting the water, drying the structure and rebuilding damaged components. Storm damage often causes several problems at once: a damaged roof lets water in, the water ruins ceilings and walls, and mold follows if nothing is dried properly. A single restoration plan from our team covers each of these related issues together.

Insurance documentation matters a great deal after a storm, so we record all damage with photographs, moisture readings, damage assessments and detailed scope-of-work reports. Signs You Need Storm Damage Repair in Nashville

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Rain blown in around door frames, window frames or openings in the wall
  • Flooding in lower levels because drainage systems were overwhelmed
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
  • Cracked caulk lets blown rain in, so inspect and renew the seals on doors and windows
  • If storm damage breaks a pipe you will need the main shutoff, so find it and practice now
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
